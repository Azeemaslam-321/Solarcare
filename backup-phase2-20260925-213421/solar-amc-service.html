AMC Coverage

What Is Included in Solar AMC?

Choose scheduled solar maintenance support for cleaner panels, clearer performance observations, and easier follow-up when your rooftop needs attention.

Scheduled Cleaning

Solar AMC starts with planned panel cleaning visits. IMSolarCare uses a non-abrasive cleaning method, soft brushes and de-ionized water so that dust, bird droppings and loose surface contamination can be removed without harsh scrubbing. Each visit is planned around roof access, water availability, panel count and safety conditions. For many Lucknow rooftops, regular cleaning is more practical than waiting until generation visibly drops or panels look heavily coated with dust.

Inspection Notes

During an AMC visit, the technician can make basic visual observations of panel glass, mounting structure, cable routing, connectors and accessible earthing points. This is not a replacement for a full electrical audit, but it helps customers notice early signs such as loose visible wiring, repeated bird activity, unusual dirt buildup, cracked glass, shading changes or inverter alerts that need a separate repair visit.

Service Records

AMC customers can request before and after service photographs, visit notes and maintenance recommendations. These records are useful for homeowners, building owners, schools, offices and facility teams who want to track when the panels were last cleaned and whether the rooftop needs a different cleaning frequency during summer, dust storms, monsoon residue or bird-heavy periods.

Residential solar AMC is designed for home rooftop systems in areas such as Gomti Nagar, Aliganj, Indira Nagar, Jankipuram, Ashiyana, Rajajipuram, Vikas Nagar, Aminabad and nearby Lucknow localities. Most home owners want a simple plan that keeps panels cleaner through the year and gives them someone to call when the inverter display, generation pattern or rooftop condition looks unusual.

The right plan depends on system size, dust exposure, bird activity, rooftop access and how often the customer wants preventive care. Homes near busy roads, construction zones or open dusty areas may need more frequent cleaning than rooftops with less exposure.

Commercial Solar AMC

Commercial solar AMC is useful for factories, warehouses, hospitals, schools, colleges, offices and institutions where rooftop solar is a business asset. Larger plants need planned service windows, repeatable cleaning routines and a clear point of contact. A 10kW, 25kW, 50kW or 100kW+ rooftop usually needs different scheduling than a small home system because panel count, access routes, roof safety and operational timings are more complex.

IMSolarCare can support commercial customers with custom cleaning frequency, site photographs, basic maintenance observations and priority coordination for recurring visits. For higher capacity systems, the AMC discussion should include plant size, number of panel rows, roof type, water access, safety requirements and whether service has to happen outside working hours.

Visit Planning

How Often Do We Visit?

Cleaning and maintenance frequency should match real site conditions instead of a fixed promise for every rooftop.

Monthly Care

Useful for dusty rooftops, bird-prone sites, commercial rooftops and systems near high-traffic or construction areas. Monthly visits help keep surface buildup from becoming difficult to remove.

Quarterly Care

A practical option for many home solar systems where dust buildup is moderate and the customer wants periodic cleaning plus visual maintenance observations.

Seasonal Care

Helpful after summer dust storms, long dry periods, bird activity, nearby construction work or monsoon residue. Seasonal visits can also be combined with inspection support.

AMC Questions

Solar AMC FAQs

Is solar AMC only for large systems?
No. Home rooftop systems can also use AMC support when the owner wants scheduled cleaning, basic inspection observations and easier follow-up throughout the year.
Does AMC include repair work?
AMC visits can identify visible issues and maintenance needs. Repair work, replacement parts or detailed electrical troubleshooting may require a separate visit or quote depending on the problem.
Can commercial sites get custom AMC schedules?
Yes. Commercial rooftops often need custom schedules based on plant capacity, roof access, safety requirements, working hours and cleaning frequency.
What details should I share before booking AMC?
Share your location, system capacity, approximate panel count, rooftop access details, recent panel condition and whether you need residential or commercial AMC support.
