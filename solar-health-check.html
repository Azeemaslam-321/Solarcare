What This Solar Health Check Covers

A solar health check is a structured observation of how your rooftop solar system is performing and what may be affecting generation. IMSolarCare checks visible panel condition, dust level, inverter display indicators, wiring condition, connectors, mounting stability and obvious safety concerns.

Visual inspection is different from advanced equipment diagnostics. If a fault needs thermal imaging, string testing or installer-level electrical testing, we clearly mention that in the service notes instead of guessing.

Solar Generation Check

We review the customer-reported generation pattern, recent drop in output, inverter reading, seasonal impact and visible site conditions. Low generation can be caused by dust, bird droppings, shading, inverter alerts, loose connections, module issues or grid-side interruptions.

  • Generation concern discussion
  • Cleaning requirement assessment
  • Visible shading and obstruction observations

Panel, Inverter and Wiring Check

The technician checks panel glass condition, visible cracks or stains, dust load, bird activity, cable routing, exposed wiring, connector condition and inverter display warnings. For safety, we do not open sealed electrical equipment unless the visit scope and site conditions allow it.

  • Panel condition check
  • Inverter indicator review
  • Wiring and connection observations

Who Needs a Health Check?

A solar system health check is useful when output suddenly drops, the inverter shows errors, bills are higher than expected, panels look dirty, a rooftop has bird activity, or the system has not been inspected for a long time. It is also useful before selecting an AMC plan.

Process and Pricing

First, IMSolarCare confirms your system size, rooftop access and main symptom. During the visit, the technician records visible observations and explains whether cleaning, maintenance, repair support or a deeper solar panel inspection is the next sensible step.

FAQs

Can cleaning fix every low-generation problem?
No. Cleaning helps when dirt or surface contamination is the cause. Inverter, wiring, shading and module faults may need maintenance or repair.

Do you provide thermal inspection?
Thermal or hotspot inspection can be recommended where applicable, especially for suspected panel faults or commercial systems.
