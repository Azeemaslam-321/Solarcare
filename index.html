Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Lucknow?
Cleaning frequency depends on dust, pollution, bird activity, weather and rooftop conditions. Dust-heavy rooftops may require more frequent cleaning.
Why use de-ionized water for solar panel cleaning?
De-ionized water helps reduce mineral residue and water spots on solar glass compared with hard water.
What is included in a solar AMC?
Solar AMC plans may include scheduled cleaning, basic inverter checks, visual inspection, electrical observations and maintenance reporting depending on the selected plan.
