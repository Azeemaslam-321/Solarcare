Why solar panel cleaning is important.

Regular cleaning helps rooftop systems stay clearer, safer to maintain, and easier to monitor over time.

Dust reduces sunlight absorption

A dusty panel surface can block light exposure and leave the system looking dull even before the problem feels serious.

Bird droppings affect panel condition

Dirty panels can reduce output

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

These are the most common questions asked before booking solar panel cleaning, maintenance availability, or commercial rooftop service.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

In dusty areas, cleaning every 15 to 30 days is usually helpful. Frequency depends on dust, pollution, and bird activity.

Does cleaning improve solar output?

Yes. Dirty panels may produce less electricity. Regular cleaning helps maintain better performance.

Is maintenance available?

Selected rooftops can enquire about maintenance availability based on service need, panel condition, and booking volume.

What if the roof is very high?

If the rooftop is at extra height or access is difficult, additional charges may apply and the client must provide a safe ladder before the visit starts.

How much time does cleaning take?

Service time depends on rooftop size, panel count, and access condition. Smaller home systems are usually quicker than larger commercial visits.

Who provides water?

Basic site water access should be available when needed for the scheduled cleaning visit.

How is price calculated?

Price depends on solar capacity, panel count, roof access, cleaning condition, and whether you need one-time service or maintenance support.

Is bird droppings cleaning included?

Basic bird waste cleanup is included where practical. Heavier buildup may need a separate deep-cleaning estimate.
