What Commercial Cleaning Includes

Commercial cleaning may include panel-safe machinery-based cleaning, surface contamination removal, bird dropping removal where practical, basic visual observations, site notes and recommendations for repeat cleaning frequency. Larger plants may need a custom cleaning schedule.

  • Commercial rooftop solar cleaning
  • Dust and surface contamination removal
  • Basic maintenance observations

Factory, Warehouse and Industrial Sites

Industrial and warehouse rooftops often face dust, smoke, traffic pollution and access challenges. Cleaning must be planned around operational hours, safety rules, panel rows, water access and rooftop movement. We quote these sites after understanding capacity and layout.

Schools, Hospitals, Offices and Hotels

Institutional and office rooftops need low-disruption visits. We can coordinate cleaning windows, access timing and reporting so facility teams know what was cleaned and what visible issues were observed during the visit.

Commercial AMC and Maintenance

For sites with repeat dust buildup, a one-time cleaning may not be enough. Commercial solar AMC can combine scheduled cleaning, basic inverter observation, bird mesh observation and maintenance notes. The final plan depends on plant size and site needs.
