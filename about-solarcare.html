Solving the Hidden Solar Output Crisis in Uttar Pradesh

In sunny regions like Lucknow, Kanpur, Ayodhya, and Varanasi, rooftop solar plants represent a significant financial investment for homeowners and commercial business owners alike. However, heavy atmospheric dust, vehicular exhaust, airborne soot, and bird droppings quickly create a sticky, opaque static film across the glass modules.

Most solar owners try cleaning panels using local municipal tap water or hard floor wipers. Unfortunately, Lucknow tap water has high mineral hardness (calcium & magnesium TDS 300-600+ PPM). When tap water bakes under 40°C UP summer heat, it leaves white mineral scale spots that permanently reduce light transmission and void panel glass warranties.

The IMSolarCare Breakthrough Solution

We pioneered mobile RO/DI (De-Ionized) water filtration units paired with telescoping soft-nylon microfiber brushes. Our ultra-pure water (<10 PPM) absorbs dust like a magnet and dries 100% spot-free, guaranteeing zero scratches and a verified 25% to 35% power boost!
