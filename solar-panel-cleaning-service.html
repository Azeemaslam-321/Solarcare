Why Ordinary Tap Water Permanently Damages Solar Glass

Groundwater in Lucknow and Uttar Pradesh contains high TDS (300-600+ PPM) of calcium and magnesium salts. Here is why tap water washing degrades your panels over time.

Tap Water White Calcification

When tap water evaporates under intense UV sunlight, it leaves hard white mineral crusts (calcium scale) on solar glass. This white scaling permanently refracts sunlight away from the PV silicon cells.

De-Ionized Soft Wash (TDS <10)

Our mobile filtration units pass water through multi-stage RO membranes and mixed-bed de-ionization resin filters. The ultra-pure water absorbs dust particles like a magnet and dries 100% spot-free.

Anti-Static Nano Rinse

Following the pure water rinse, we apply an anti-static hydrophobic treatment that reduces dust adhesion. Dry soot and sand slide off during light rain, keeping panels clean 2x longer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cleaning Service FAQs

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Lucknow?
In dust-heavy areas like Lucknow and Uttar Pradesh, cleaning every 15 to 30 days is recommended to maintain peak generation. Seasonal dust storms (Aandhi) in summer require immediate washing.
Will washing solar panels void my manufacturer warranty?
No! We use soft non-abrasive microfiber nylon brushes and soft de-ionized water, strictly following tier-1 panel manufacturers' guidelines (Waaree, Adani, Tata Solar, Vikram Solar).
Do I need to be at home during the cleaning service?
As long as our technicians have safe access to your rooftop and a standard electrical outlet for our water filtration kit, you do not need to pause your routine.
