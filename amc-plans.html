How to Choose a Plan

Choose a lower-frequency plan if your rooftop stays relatively clean and access is simple. Choose more frequent visits if your site has road dust, bird activity, nearby construction, commercial usage or a larger system where downtime matters.

What Plans Can Include

  • Scheduled cleaning visits
  • Basic inverter visual check
  • Panel condition and dirt-level observations
  • Priority booking
  • Service notes after each visit

Pricing Notes

AMC pricing depends on system size, panel quantity, rooftop access, cleaning frequency and whether extra inspection or repair support is required. Parts, electrical repair and replacement work are not included unless quoted separately.

FAQs

Is AMC better than one-time cleaning?
AMC is better when panels get dirty repeatedly or you want scheduled care.

Can commercial sites take AMC?
Yes. Commercial solar AMC is quoted after reviewing plant size and access.

Can I start with one visit?
Yes. Many customers begin with cleaning or health check, then move to AMC.
