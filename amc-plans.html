AMC FAQ

Solar AMC questions customers usually ask

Does AMC include solar panel cleaning?

No. Solar panel cleaning is separately chargeable. AMC plans cover inspection, support and maintenance checks based on the selected plan.

Can I get a final AMC quote directly?

Final quotation is confirmed after checking solar system size, site location, rooftop access and current system condition.

Are spare parts included?

No. Spare parts, replacement components, cable replacement, earthing repair, inverter repair and major structural work are separately chargeable.

Which AMC plan is best for homes?

Basic works for simple yearly follow-up. Standard is better for most homes and small commercial rooftops because it includes 4 visits and one emergency inspection.
