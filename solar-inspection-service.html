What Is Checked During Inspection?

A solar panel inspection looks at rooftop condition, panel glass, frame condition, mounting, cable routing, visible connector condition, inverter display indicators and cleaning condition. It is useful when a system is old, recently shifted, underperforming or due for maintenance.

When Inspection Is Needed

Book a solar inspection service in Lucknow if your solar generation is low, the inverter shows warning lights, panels have visible marks, mounting looks loose, wiring is exposed, or the system has not been checked after storms, construction dust or bird activity.

  • Low-generation symptoms
  • Visible damage or loose mounting
  • Post-monsoon or post-construction check

Safety and Electrical Observations

Our inspection includes visual safety observations around cable management, exposed wires, connector condition, earthing visibility and inverter indicators. Electrical repair or internal equipment diagnosis may need a separate repair visit or qualified installer-level testing.

Panels, Mounting and Cleaning Condition

Panels are checked for dirt layer, bird droppings, stains, visible cracks, frame corrosion and uneven soiling. Mounting observations include loose-looking clamps, rust, roof access limitations and areas where maintenance may be unsafe without proper preparation.

Inspection vs Health Check

A health check focuses on overall performance symptoms and next-step advice. A solar panel inspection is more structured around physical condition, rooftop safety observations and service readiness. For many homes, both can happen during the same visit when scope is clear.
