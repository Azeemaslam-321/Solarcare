Cleaning, Inspection, Maintenance, AMC and Repair

Solar cleaning removes dust and surface contamination. Inspection checks visible condition, wiring observations and inverter indicators. Maintenance combines cleaning, checks and minor service observations. AMC is a planned recurring maintenance schedule. Repair is for specific faults, parts or electrical troubleshooting.

What Maintenance Includes

Solar panel maintenance in Lucknow may include panel cleaning, cleaning requirement assessment, inverter display check, visible wiring observation, connector and cable routing observation, bird activity notes, mounting condition observations and service recommendations.

Who Needs Preventive Maintenance?

Maintenance is useful for homes and commercial rooftops where generation is gradually dropping, panels are exposed to road dust, bird droppings are common, the inverter shows occasional alerts, or the system has not been checked for several months.

No. Maintenance is preventive care and observations; repair addresses specific faults or parts.

Can I convert maintenance to AMC?
Yes, after the first visit you can choose a recurring AMC plan if it fits your system.
