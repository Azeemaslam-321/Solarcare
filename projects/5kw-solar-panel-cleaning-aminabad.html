Aminabad Case Study

5kW Rooftop Solar Panel Cleaning in Aminabad

This project shows a residential rooftop solar cleaning visit in Aminabad, Lucknow. The site had visible surface dust and bird-related dirt, so the customer needed a careful cleaning visit and practical maintenance observations.

Capacity: 5kWLocation: AminabadService: Panel cleaning

Customer's Solar Panel Condition

The rooftop panels had accumulated dust and visible marks that made the glass surface look dull. Aminabad rooftops can also face nearby road dust and bird activity, so the cleaning plan focused on removing loose dirt and surface contamination without abrasive tools.

Cleaning Process

The IMSolarCare team used a non-abrasive cleaning method with soft brushes and de-ionized water. The visit included panel-by-panel cleaning, a visible check of the surrounding rooftop area, and before/after photo documentation for the customer.

Before and After Cleaning

After cleaning, the panels had a clearer glass surface and were easier for the customer to visually inspect. Actual generation improvement varies depending on panel condition, dust accumulation, weather and system performance, so we describe the result as surface restoration rather than a fixed output promise.

Project Details

  • Panel capacity: 5kW residential rooftop
  • Location: Aminabad, Lucknow
  • Problem: visible dust and surface contamination
  • Work done: de-ionized water cleaning and visual observations
  • Result: cleaner panel surface and better maintenance visibility
