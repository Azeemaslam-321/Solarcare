Solar Panel Cleaning in Kamta, Lucknow

This Kamta Chauraha project shows a local rooftop cleaning visit where the panel surface had visible dust buildup. The service focused on safe cleaning, surface clarity and practical advice for repeat care.

Location: Kamta ChaurahaService: Rooftop cleaningPhotos: before and after

Customer's Solar Panel Condition

The rooftop panels had visible dust and surface marks before service. In areas around Kamta and Faizabad Road, road dust, open surroundings and delayed rooftop cleaning can make panels look dull and harder to inspect.

Cleaning Process

The cleaning team used a non-abrasive process with soft tools and de-ionized water. The work included careful panel surface cleaning, basic rooftop observations and after-cleaning photographs so the customer could see the visible difference.

Before and After Cleaning

The cleaned panels showed a clearer surface after service. The practical result for the customer was easier visual inspection and a better baseline for future maintenance. Generation improvement depends on the panel condition, dust level, weather and system performance.

Project Details

  • Location: Kamta Chauraha, Lucknow
  • Problem: dust buildup and dull panel surface
  • Work done: de-ionized water solar panel cleaning
  • Result: clearer panel surface after service
  • Next step: recurring cleaning frequency based on site condition
